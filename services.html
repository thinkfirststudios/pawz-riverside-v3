What We Do

Pawz Riverside Pet Services

Pet Sitting

One-on-one in-home care for your pet while you're away. Whether you need daily visits or overnight stays, we make sure your pet is happy, comfortable, and loved. Custom packages available for long-term or multi-pet situations — contact us for a quote.

Pet Taxi Service

Safe, reliable transportation to your pet's most important appointments. We offer one-way or round-trip transport to veterinary visits and grooming appointments. A vet chaperone option lets us stay with your pet throughout their entire appointment so you never have to worry.

Dog Walking

Keep your dog healthy and happy with regular walks from an experienced local walker. Every outing brings exercise, engagement, and playtime. Choose from a 20-minute Quick Stretch drop-in or a full Daily Trek hour-long adventure through Riverside's best routes.
