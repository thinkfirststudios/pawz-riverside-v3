Our Story

About Us

Our Mission

More Than Just a Walk in the Park

At Pawz Riverside, we believe every pet deserves a day filled with adventure, safety, and love. Our mission is to provide Riverside and Corona's furry residents with premium care that gives owners total peace of mind. Whether it's a high-energy hike or a cozy afternoon visit, we treat your pets like members of our own family.

Rooted in the Community

Founded by a lifelong animal lover, Pawz Riverside started with a simple goal: to create a reliable, local solution for busy pet parents. We know the best trails, the quietest parks, and exactly how much a belly rub matters. Proud to serve Riverside and Corona and the pets that make it home.

Professional Care You Can Trust

I don't just watch dogs, I engage them. Trained in pet safety and behavior to ensure every interaction is positive and secure. I communicate with pet owners regularly so you always know how your furry family member is doing, even when you're far from home.

Our Values

What guides everything we do

Compassion First

Every decision centers around empathy: for your pets and for you. Animal wellbeing comes first, always.

Safety Always

Trained in pet behavior and first aid basics. We don't take risks with the animals in our care.

Reliable & On Time

When you book with us, we show up. No last-minute cancellations, no no-shows. Your trust means everything.
